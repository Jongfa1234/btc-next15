# BTC Next 15

A single web page that gives a probabilistic call on whether Bitcoin will be higher or lower in 15 minutes, using free public exchange data (Coinbase first, Binance.US and Binance as backups).

It trains a small logistic-regression model in your browser on about 90 days of 15-minute candles, shows a 30-day walk-forward out-of-sample test, and keeps your own track record in your browser.

**Not financial advice.** Nobody can reliably predict 15-minute price moves. Expect hit rates near 50%.
