How it works

It downloads about 90 days of 15-minute BTC-USD candles from Coinbase (Binance.US, then Binance, are the backups). Coinbase candles don't split buyers from sellers, so that share comes from Binance.US for the same 15 minutes. When reachable, it also pulls perpetual-futures funding rates (OKX or Binance) and the Binance futures premium over spot. Each source that fails is skipped and listed on the page.

For every candle it measures momentum over 15 minutes, 1 hour and 4 hours, RSI, a short vs. long moving-average gap, where the price sits in its recent range, volume spikes, time of day, and trade flow (the share of volume from buyers hitting the market). With futures data it adds the funding rate and the futures premium. A logistic-regression model learns from the last 60 days how those relate to the next candle going up or down.

The out-of-sample test covers the most recent 30 days. The model is never shown the day it is judged on. The "luck range" is where a pure coin flip would land 95% of the time with that many calls. A hit rate inside it is not evidence of a real edge.
